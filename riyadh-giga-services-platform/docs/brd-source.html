1Executive Summary

Riyadh is delivering one of the largest coordinated urban-development programs in the world. Giga projects such as Diriyah, Qiddiya, New Murabba, King Salman International Airport and Expo 2030 Riyadh depend on hundreds of municipal decisions — survey decisions, plan approvals, building permits, infrastructure handovers, environmental clearances and operating licenses — whose timeliness directly gates construction schedules measured in billions of riyals.

The Giga-Projects Municipal Services Platform (منصة الخدمات البلدية للمشاريع الكبرى) is a single, bilingual gateway through which giga-project entities, developers, engineering offices, investors and operators submit, track and receive every municipal service relevant to a large project — against published service-level agreements (SLAs), with structured escalation, an auditable decision trail, and live measurement of economic impact.

A fully interactive prototype has been built and styled as a native section of the official municipality portal, alriyadh.gov.sa. This BRD records the business requirements embodied by that prototype and specifies the requirements for integrating the platform into the official portal: placement inside الخدمات الإلكترونية, unified sign-on (Nafath and portal accounts), the shared design system, unified notifications, the 940 contact channel, hosting, analytics, and compliance obligations.

2Background and Strategic Context

Vision 2030 and the Riyadh agenda. National strategy positions Riyadh among the world's ten largest city economies, with population targets approaching 15 million and a giga-project investment pipeline spanning culture, sport, aviation, housing and urban regeneration. Municipal enablement — permits, plans, inspections, licenses — is on the critical path of that pipeline.

The Giga Projects Office. The Amanah has established a dedicated Giga Projects Office (مكتب المشاريع الكبرى) as the single municipal counterpart for large projects: one registry of projects, one prioritized lane for their requests, one escalation route for cross-agency blockages (التحديات), and one accountable scoreboard of timeliness and impact.

The digital gap. Today these interactions are dispersed across general-purpose channels that were not designed for the scale, urgency or multi-entity coordination that giga projects require. There is no unified view of a project's municipal pipeline, no published giga-specific SLAs, and no measurement of the economic cost of delay.

Why integrate with alriyadh.gov.sa. The official portal is the Amanah's front door: it carries the trusted brand, the unified design system, national sign-on, the 940 channel, the myCity app ecosystem and portal governance. Launching the platform as a portal section — rather than a stand-alone site — inherits that trust and infrastructure from day one, and keeps a single citizen- and business-facing digital estate.

4Scope

4.1 In scope

  • Public section (inside the portal): platform landing with the services catalog (دليل الخدمات) and the interactive developer/investor journeys (رحلات المطور والمستثمر العقاري).
  • Customer portal: guided request wizard, my-requests tracking, journey canvas with live progress, resubmission, challenges, notifications, satisfaction rating, profile and delegation.
  • Staff workspace: triage queue, three-pane review workspace, document verification, external referrals, decisions with conditions/co-sign, SLA clocks and escalation.
  • Management: GPO dashboard, projects registry, KPIs, reports, service-catalog administration, audit log, settings (holidays, SLA policy), impact dashboard (أثر المنصة).
  • Executive view: boardroom summary with impact headline figures and weekly highlights.
  • Portal integration: identity, design system, hosting, notifications, 940/chat, myCity, open data, analytics (Section 9).

4.2 Out of scope (this phase)

  • Fee payment execution (SADAD integration) — fees are displayed; collection remains on existing rails until Phase 2.
  • Automated GIS parcel validation and field-inspection scheduling (Phase 2 with Amanah GIS).
  • Direct system-to-system integrations with external entities (water, power, environment authorities) beyond the referral workflow — Phase 2 via the Government Service Bus.
  • Non-giga (general public) municipal services — these remain in the portal's existing e-services.
Prototype status. The current build demonstrates the full experience with fictional data on a client-side data store by design. Its data layer is a swappable repository interface so that Phase 1 replaces storage with Amanah CRM/REST services without UI change.

7Proposed Solution Overview

One platform, five experiences, one data spine:

ModuleWhat it does
Services catalog (دليل الخدمات)38 documented services across the project lifecycle — pre-development (planning, survey decisions, master plans), during delivery (building/demolition permits, infrastructure design and handover, environmental construction permits, enablement permits such as cranes, hoarding, road closures, dewatering, utility relocation), and post-completion (operating licenses, activity licensing, periodic compliance, renewals). Each service publishes purpose, prerequisites, required documents with accepted formats, fees model, supporting entities and SLA in working days.
Journeys (رحلات المطور والمستثمر)Five official journeys rendered as an interactive canvas of connected stages (serpentine on desktop, spine on mobile): planning (7 steps and three plan-approval paths), building (5), investor (6), operation & asset management (7), industrial (5). Signed-in customers see their project's live position («أنت هنا»), completed and blocked stages, and can start any service from its stage.
Request wizardGuided, validating intake: project and phase selection, dynamic service forms, document upload with format checks, review and declaration, drafts, and a tracking number on submission.
Lifecycle engineTwelve request states from draft to closure with role-guarded transitions; SLA clocks on a Sunday–Thursday calendar with official holidays; the clock pauses while a request is returned to the customer; escalation bands (green/amber/red) with automatic GPO escalation at breach; fast-track lane halving SLAs for giga entities; co-sign rule for fee-bearing or fast-track decisions; permit numbering on approval.
Review workspaceThree-pane specialist screen: request summary and SLA clock; document verification checklist; decision pane with approve/return/reject/refer, conditions, regulation references and templates.
Challenges (التحديات)Structured intake of cross-agency blockages tied to a project and optionally a request; categories, owner assignment, SLA, escalation to GPO coordination, resolution record.
Impact (أثر المنصة)Delay-cost model computed live from decided requests: working days saved vs. pre-platform baselines, estimated savings (SAR), investment value accelerated, estimated GDP contribution, jobs supported — with a per-request impact ledger, methodology disclosure, and honest estimated/measured labeling (Section 11).
Reporting & auditKPI engine (processing time, SLA compliance, first response, on-time rate, challenge closure, satisfaction), weekly trends, printable executive brief, and a full audit log of administrative and decision actions.
Experience layerArabic-first RTL with full English mirror; the portal's design system (Section 9); accessibility and mobile hardening; command palette; theme/accent/density personalization.

11Data, Reporting and the Impact Model

The platform measures itself. Six operational KPIs are computed live (average processing time, SLA compliance, first-response time, on-time rate incl. open requests, challenge closure, satisfaction), and an economic impact model converts time saved into money terms using a delay-cost methodology:

  • Every service carries a documented pre-platform baseline duration; every project carries an indicative investment value and a daily delay-cost factor; every service carries a critical-path weight.
  • For each approved request: days saved = baseline − actual net working days; savings = days saved × project value × daily factor × critical-path weight; accelerated value and GDP/jobs contributions derive from standard economic coefficients.
  • Every figure is labeled estimated (modeled) or measured (directly observed); the methodology is published in full inside the product, and a per-request impact ledger makes headline numbers auditable.
In production, baselines, project values and coefficients shall be replaced with officially approved figures, and the model reviewed by the Amanah's economics function before external publication (see R-05).
