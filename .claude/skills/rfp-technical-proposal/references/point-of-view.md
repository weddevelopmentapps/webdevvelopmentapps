# Section Playbook — وجهة نظرنا (Our Point of View)

The longest bespoke section (~37–42 slides) and the proposal's soul: it proves we
understand the client's world *better than the RFP describes it*, then argues from that
world to the exact work we will propose. Two movements: the **context funnel** and the
**argument arc**. Every slide title is an assertion; read consecutively they form a
continuous essay (see `reference-storylines.md` for both full sequences).

---

## Movement 1 — The context funnel (world → kingdom → city → client → sub-unit)

**The funnel law**: zoom in step by step, each slide answering "why does the next,
narrower thing matter?", and STOP at the exact organizational altitude of the RFP scope.
Scope = whole entity → land on the "backbone" slide. Scope = a sub-unit → keep
narrowing: entity strategy → entity ecosystem → territorial/programmatic decomposition →
sub-unit org chart → sub-unit services. The funnel's tail slides carry ~100% of the new
client research.

**The narrowing device**: `تركيز الشرائح التالية` — a small green tag + **dashed-green
rounded frame** drawn around the one element of a diagram that the following slides zoom
into. This device physically executes the funnel (client chip on the stakeholder wall →
"5 قطاعات" card → the focus sector on the map). Use it at every narrowing step.

### Canonical funnel beats (D1 slides 8–18 ≙ D2 8–17 + tail 18–22)

1. **Cities-through-history opener** (split slide; photo panel = 4 white prose ¶s each
   with ONE green-bold phrase; white panel = hub-and-spoke of 12 city-benefit icon
   chips connected by a thin teal `0D8390` all-to-all web). 100% reusable boilerplate.
2. **KSA macro-economy** (full-bleed satellite photo; white 3-scenario GDP line chart,
   white ellipse chips `CAGR = 8%/5.4%/3%`; green `43B02A` wedge callout `هدف رؤية 2030
   = 6.5 ترليون ريال` pointing to a green dot at 2030; left stat ladder `4 – 6.2
   ترليون ريال في عام 2030`). Title's green tail: `…والذي سيعتمد بشكل رئيسي على نمو
   المناطق والمدن`.
3. **Vision-2030 cascade pyramid** (title ends `...`; official Vision logo; 3 levels of
   boxes — 3 pillars → 4 L2 objectives → 5 L3 objectives, SELECTED for relevance to the
   RFP domain, hence the mandatory `غير شامل` pill; bottom band of 3 circled national
   targets. Source: `المصدر: رؤية 2030`).
4. **Royal quote slide** (continuation title `... وهي ركيزة اساسية لنمو المملكة…`;
   full black background; cut-out portrait of the Crown Prince right; giant bold white
   quote with oversized quotation-mark graphics; the target sentence green-bold
   (`ونستهدف أن تكون الرياض من أكبر 10 اقتصاديات مدن في العالم`); attribution: bold
   name + title. NEVER paraphrase a royal quote; no source line — the speaker is the
   source. For non-Riyadh work find a verbatim royal/ministerial quote naming the
   client's domain.)
5. **City credentials cascade** (OPTIONAL — D1 only): 3 columns headed by dark-green
   pills that chain into one sentence; share-stats (27% / 50% / 38%), ranking rows with
   real IMD/Savills/GaWC logos, 6 initiative chips. Drop when the funnel needs room.
6. **Region population twin-title slide**: white panel = region choropleth
   (single-hue green ramp, per-governorate `{عدد} {الاسم}` labels, gradient legend
   `كثافة سكانية منخفضة/عالية`) + dark middle stat ladder (`8.6 مليون`, `23 نسمة لكل
   كم²`, `81%` — white numbers here) + photo panel with the green continuation title
   `... 81% منهم يتركزون في مدينة الرياض`. **Echo trick**: the same headline number
   appears in both the green title and the stat ladder.
7. **District choropleth** ("we know your streets"): ~75% real GIS heat map of city
   districts (5-bin ramp + legend card `مفتاح الخريطة`), title-as-paragraph naming the
   top-3 districts in prose, inset green bar chart `اعلى الأحياء في عدد السكان`.
   Requires real GIS output — a schematic map fails the authenticity bar.
8. **Future-problem scenario slide**: population scenarios to 2040 (same white-line
   chart grammar), big `23~ مليون نسمة` green stat; left dark panel = green/white mixed
   continuation headline + **3 bold-lead implication bullets that stealth-restate the
   RFP's service pillars in its own vocabulary** — this is where context welds to scope.
9. **Value-chain × services matrix** (Deloitte IP slide): 6 gray chevron value-chain
   headers (RTL), 14–15 green service-group bars (green categorical ramp dark→bright by
   family), center white card `420 خدمة عبر 14 مجموعة خدمات`, right rail of 3
   family cards. Source cites `إطار ديلويت لإدارة المدن`. Recount services per client.
10. **Stakeholder logo wall** (continuation title `... من خلال أكثر من 100+ صاحب
    مصلحة…`): white rounded logo cards bucketed by governance layer (مركز الحكومة →
    برامج الرؤية → 16 وزارة → هيئات وصناديق → شركات → خاص/ثالث), right rail of 4 green
    count chips (`14+ جهة مركزية`…), `غير شامل` pill, and the dashed-green focus frame
    on the client's chip. Real logos, consistent sizes.
11. **Backbone landing** (funnel destination): title `تشكل {الجهة} العامود الاساسي في
    التنمية الشاملة، وتعمل كجهة ممكنة ومحركة للنهضة الشاملة…`; left dark stat ladder
    counting the client's strategy (`10 أهداف استراتيجية`, `26+ مبادرة`, `44+ مؤشر
    أداء`, `4 ممكنات`) with big green numbers; right = the client's **strategy house**
    (roof = الرؤية verbatim, mission bar, 4 pillar columns, values base) built from
    their published strategy; `*` footnote band to preempt staleness objections.
    Source: `المصدر: {الجهة}`.

### Sub-unit tail (when scope is a division/sector — D2 pattern)

12. **Strategy-linkage hub**: client logo in center green circle; 6 white satellite
    cards each = real document screenshots + big number + `{X}+ مستهدف ذات علاقة في
    {الاستراتيجية}` — counting linkage to Vision 2030, national strategies, city
    strategy, transformation programs. Use REAL page screenshots.
13. **Ecosystem canopy**: green umbrella arc over 3 category columns (bright-green
    header bars + white cards with counts/logos/ownership %); dashed focus frame on the
    RFP-relevant category.
14. **Territorial decomposition map**: real street-grid map, sectors colored — **brand
    green ONLY on the focus sector**, muted pastels elsewhere; mega stats (`5 قطاعات`,
    `16 بلدية`); focus frame + tag on the scope sector.
15. **Sub-unit org chart** (white slide): title states totals (`يتكون قطاع الوسط من
    22+ إدارة و32+ قسم`); 3-level tree — dark-green bands = line leadership, gray
    rounded boxes = units, small white chips = section counts (`3 أقسام`) instead of
    drawing every box. Counts in title must equal the chart.
16. **Domains × services rows** (closes the funnel onto scope): title `ويعمل {القطاع}
    على تلبية الاحتياجات… من خلال 5 مجالات رئيسية وأكثر من 14 خدمة رئيسية` (green on
    the counts); 5 icon+chip domain rows → green arrows → translucent service tiles;
    bottom white band `++ خدمات أخرى يقدمها القطاع بشكل مستمر`. This taxonomy must
    RECUR in the approach section — it is the bridge out of context into methodology.

### Funnel data discipline

- Chart headers: bold, `{المقياس} ({السنوات}، {الوحدة})` — e.g. `سيناريوهات نمو الناتج
  المحلي الإجمالي الحقيقي للمملكة العربية السعودية (2021 – 2040F)، ترليون ريال`; axis
  unit repeated as a small label above the y-axis.
- Source lines: `المصدر: أبحاث وتحليلات مونيتور ديلويت، {الجهة}، الهيئة العامة للإحصاء`
  — Deloitte research first, then client, then public statistical bodies.
- Stat forms: `X+`, ranges `4 – 6.2`, approx `23~`. In titles the doubling `أكثر من
  100+` is a house quirk.
- The 10-slide Riyadh funnel is a frozen library for Riyadh clients (refresh data years);
  for any other city, rebuild every data beat with the same grammar.

## Movement 2 — The argument arc (needs → areas → methods → proof → one team)

Slide count ~20–27. The beats:

1. **The needs frame**: `ولمواكبة التطور والتنمية في المدينة، تحتاج {الجهة} إلى 3
   ممكنات رئيسية` (opportunity-framing) or `3 تحديات تشغيلية تواجه {القطاع}`
   (problem-framing). 3-card rail, all cards lit (archetype E in
   `slide-archetypes.md`). Exactly 3; MECE; each provable and each foreshadowing scoped
   work.
2. **Per-need statement + evidence** (×3): statement slide (spotlight rail — active
   card lit, siblings dimmed; title `الممكن الأول: {…}` / `التحدي الثاني: {…}`)
   followed by an evidence slide whose title often continues the thought with `...`
   (`تشهد مدينة الرياض تحول متسارع تقوده ...`, `حجم منظومة قطاع وسط الرياض ...`),
   carrying researched data that proves the need.
3. **The pivot**: `ولتحقيق الممكنات، ينبغي أن تعمل {الجهة} على {N} مجالات رئيسية` —
   the N work areas (numbered) mapping 1:1 onto approach tracks. The hinge of the whole
   proposal; the needs→areas→tracks chain must be traceable by an evaluator.
4. **Methodology previews** (~6–12 slides): per work area, 1–3 slides teasing the
   Deloitte method that delivers it (policy cycle, analytics, TOM elements, KPI
   dashboards, scenario planning, knowledge transfer…), pulled from the frameworks
   library but *contextualized*: titles assert what the method does FOR THIS PROJECT;
   include one worked example (`مثال عن دراسة عقارية في المملكة…`); tag mockups
   `للتوضيح`.
5. **The proof block** (~4–7 slides):
   - Synthesis: `فهمنا العميق لتطلعات {الجهة} بالإضافة إلى خبرتنا الفريدة… سيمكّناننا
     من تلبية الاحتياجات`.
   - Quantified familiarity: `عملنا على أكثر من 8 عروض تنفيذية للقيادة العليا…`،
     `كما عملنا على 15+ مشروع على مستوى المدينة` — project walls tagged `غير شامل`.
   - Named success stories: `لدى ديلويت 15+ قصة نجاح في {الخدمة} مع جهات محلية` + 2–3
     mini-credential slides with client logos.
   - Alliance slide when partners are bid: `يتمتع التحالف المقترح برؤية وقدرات شاملة…`.
   - Expert-insight slide: `ستتمكن {الجهة} من الاستفادة من المعارف والمرئيات الموجودة
     لدى مجموعة من الخبراء المختصين الرائدين في قطاعاتهم`.
6. **One-team closing** (always the section's last slide): `واخيراً، سنعمل كفريق واحد
   مع {الجهة} لتحقيق الاهداف والأثر المرجو من المشروع` — panoramic city photo, 4
   numbered value statements with giant `D0D0CE` ghost numerals.

## Authoring order

1. Write the **title sequence first** — all ~35 titles as a continuous essay
   (و/ولذلك/كما connectors; `...` chains). Fix the argument BEFORE building slides.
2. Slot each title into an archetype; mark which need `تركيز الشرائح التالية` frames.
3. Research every data beat (number + source per slide).
4. Build; verify needs→areas→tracks mapping against the approach chapter.

## What makes this section human-grade

Real GIS maps and real document screenshots as evidence; counts in titles reconcile
with counts drawn on the slide; one assertion per slide with a single green so-what
phrase; ellipsis chains make consecutive slides one continuous sentence; dashed focus
frames physically narrow the funnel; dense 10–13pt diagram labels on a strict grid.
