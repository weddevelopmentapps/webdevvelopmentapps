## 0. THE ARC — slide-by-slide map (mirrored across both decks)

The PoV argument is a fixed 5-movement rhetorical machine:

| Movement | DECK1 | DECK2 | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Diagnosis overview (N drivers) | s19 «ولمواكبة التطور والتنمية في المدينة، تحتاج الأمانة إلى 3 ممكنات رئيسية» | s23 «3 تحديات تشغيلية تواجه قطاع الوسط» | Assert N=3 named drivers on 3 photo cards |
| B. Driver deep-dive ×3 (STATEMENT+EVIDENCE pairs) | s20/21, s22/23, s24/25 (الممكن الأول/الثاني/الثالث) | s24/25, s26/27, s28/29 (التحدي الأول/الثاني/الثالث) | Spotlight one driver, then prove it with data |
| C. Pivot to work areas | s26 «ولتحقيق الممكنات، ينبغي أن تعمل الأمانة على 4 مجالات رئيسية» | s30 «ينبغي أن يعمل قطاع الوسط على 3 مجالات رئيسية لمعالجة التحديات وتحقيق النجاح التشغيلي» | Convert drivers into N numbered work areas = future approach tracks |
| D. Methodology previews (tab-tracked) | s27–35 (policy framework, guides, risk, report methodology, real-estate case, knowledge transfer, scenario planning) | s31–42 (TOM 10 elements, BPMN, RASCI, governance, EVM, KPI dashboards, PM framework, report types, dashboard examples 1/3–3/3) | Tease Deloitte IP per work area, each slide tagged to its area via tab bar |
| E. Proof & closing | s36 (4 differentiators) → s37 (8+ exec briefs) → s38 (ways of working) → s39 (8 RRM projects) → s40 (15+ city projects) → s41 (alliance) → s42 (200+ experts) → s43 (one team) | s43 (15+ success stories) → s44 (ministry 15 وكالة /17 أمانة) → s45 (24 KPI × 17 amanah) → s46 (8 RRM projects, verbatim reuse) → s47 (one team) | Credentials laddered from most specific (this client) to broadest, then emotional close |

Grammar note: every movement-opening title begins with a connective — «ولمواكبة...» (and to keep pace), «ولتحقيق الممكنات...» (and to achieve the enablers), «وسنعمل...»/«واخيراً، سنعمل...» — the deck literally reads as ONE running sentence.

DECK1 frames drivers positively as ممكنات (enablers) because it sells an aspirational advisory retainer; DECK2 frames them as تحديات (challenges) because it sells an operational fix. Same skeleton, opposite valence — choose by RFP mood.

## 1. ARCHETYPE A — "N drivers" OVERVIEW slide (D1 s19, D2 s23)

**Narrative role:** the thesis slide. Declares that exactly N (=3) forces require action, naming all three before any detail. Everything until the pivot slide hangs off these three phrases.

**Layout anatomy (identical EMU geometry across both decks — this is template):**
- Full-bleed dark city-photo background with heavy dark scrim.
- Title zone at top (~0.95in tall), title white, right-aligned RTL.
- 3 photo cards, each **3682637 × 4847280 EMU (3.03 × 3.99 in)**, y=1161336 (0.95in), x = 498475 / 4295955 / 8093437 → ~0.09in gutters. RTL order: card 1 (الممكن الأول) is the RIGHTMOST card.
- Each card: its own photo fill (chess hand, sculpture, person-with-arrows in D1; graph screen, city dusk, traffic jam in D2) + dark gradient scrim (alpha stack 74000/93000/98000 seen in XML) so white text passes.
- Icon badge: **teal filled circle 1172794 EMU (0.96in) with white line-art icon, inside a broken white ring 1423803 EMU (1.17in)** (ring drawn as arc with a gap — signature style). Teal = accent5 0D8390.
- Under badge: card title, then a **green underline rule (86BC25), line shape ~2766820 EMU (2.27in) wide**, then body paragraph.

**Typography:**
- Slide title: Sakkal Majalla, 28–32pt (sz 2800/3200 in XML), white FFFFFF, regular (not bold), right-aligned.
- Card titles: Sakkal Majalla bold 20pt (D1) / 24pt (D2), white.
- Card body: Sakkal Majalla 16pt (sz 1600), white, centered within card.
- Footer 8pt (sz 800).

**Title formula:** [connective]+[context clause]، تحتاج [client] إلى [N] ممكنات رئيسية / [N] تحديات تشغيلية تواجه [client]. The digit is always a numeral ("3", not "ثلاثة").

**Body pattern per card:** 1 sentence, 20–30 words, structure = current dynamic + implied need: «تشهد مدينة الرياض وتيرة تطور غير مسبوقة تقودها المشاريع الكبرى، ما يفرض اتخاذ قرارات ذات أثر ممتد ويتطلب مستوى عالي من التحليل والدراسة لصنع القرار». Card titles are noun phrases 4–7 words: «التعمق في فهم التوجهات الاستراتيجية للمدينة», «مواءمة دور القطاع مع التوجه الاستراتيجي للأمانة».

**D2-only micro-icons:** traffic light / architecture / circular-arrows solid glyphs (Graphic59/61/66) — icons are semantically matched to each challenge.

## 2. ARCHETYPE B — STATEMENT slide (الممكن الأول/الثاني/الثالث · التحدي الأول/الثاني/الثالث)
(D1 s20, s22, s24; D2 s24, s26, s28)

**Narrative role:** a beat of emphasis. NOT a new layout — it is Archetype A duplicated with a spotlight: the two non-focus cards get a near-black overlay (alpha ~74–80%) so only the focused card stays lit. Creates a "carousel" feel when paging.

**Anatomy:**
- Title rewritten to: **«الممكن الأول: [exact card title]»** / **«التحدي الثاني: [exact card title]»** — ordinal + colon + verbatim repetition of the card headline. No paraphrase, ever. (There is no separate numbered badge shape — the ordinal lives only in the title; the visual "badge" is the lit teal icon circle itself.)
- Focused card identical to overview (teal circle 0D8390, white ring arc, green 86BC25 underline, 16pt body).
- Dimmed cards remain faintly legible (ghosting = intentional context retention).
- Spotlight order follows RTL: first = right card, second = middle, third = left.
- XML of D2 s24: schemeClr accent1 ×41 (86BC25 underlines etc.), accent5 ×9 (teal), overlay alphas 24000/74000/76078/93000/98000. Colors srgb: 86BC25, 00332A (deep green-black scrim tint), FFFFFF.

**Typography:** title 28pt (D1) / 24pt (D2) Sakkal Majalla white; everything else inherited.

**Reconstruction recipe:** build Archetype A once; per driver, clone the slide, prepend «الممكن/التحدي ال{أول|ثاني|ثالث}: » to the title, drop a black ~75%-alpha rounded rect over each non-focus card. 3 drivers → 3 statement slides. Zero new content is written for these slides — that is the trick.

## 3. ARCHETYPE C — EVIDENCE slide (the "..." continuation pair)
(D1 s21, s23, s25; D2 s25, s27, s29)

**Narrative role:** proves the statement just made. Its title is grammatically CONTINUOUS with itself via ellipses: a right-half assertion that trails off + a left-half consequence that begins with «...».

**Split-title convention (the signature move):**
- Right (white panel) title, dark gray/black 28pt: an observation ending open: «تشهد مدينة الرياض تحول متسارع تقوده ...» / «حجم منظومة قطاع وسط الرياض ...» / «يتوزع دور الأمانة على عدد من الوكالات والإدارات...»
- Left (dark panel) kicker, white 28pt, starts with dots: «.... مما يتطلب تعزيز قدرات التخطيط واتخاذ القرار المبكر» / «... يفرض الحاجة لإدارة الأداء بكفاءة من خلال نموذج تشغيلي مستدام» / «... بما يتطلب خبرات استراتيجية واقتصادية متكاملة تغطي كافة الأدوار»
- Connectives rotate: مما يتطلب / بما يتطلب / ما يفرض الحاجة لـ / يفرض متابعة وإشراف مستمر. FACT ... → ... THEREFORE-CAPABILITY. The left kicker is always the *requirement* that Deloitte will later sell.

**Layout anatomy (~42/58 vertical split):**
- RIGHT ≈58%: white background = the DATA half. Contains 1–3 numbered mini-sections with small green square/badge numerals (1, 2), stat blocks, logo strips, mini org-charts, callout boxes.
- LEFT ≈42%: dark bokeh-photo panel = the IMPLICATION half. 3–4 rows, each = white outline line-art icon (right side of row, ~0.55in circle or bare glyph) + one bold 28pt sentence, rows separated by thin white hairlines. Key 2–4-word phrases inside each sentence are colored GREEN — XML: **schemeClr accent4 (046A38) + lumMod 60000/lumOff 40000 → renders ≈ bright leaf green**, bold, e.g. «قرارات طويلة المدى», «تخطيطًا حضريًا», «دراسة المخاطر والفرص», «اتخاذ قرارات سريعة ومرنة», «تحويل التوصيات المعتمدة إلى توجهات تنفيذية».
- Chevron connectors: white "double bracket" arrows sitting ON the panel boundary pointing from data → implication (D1 s21) or a large green two-way arrow ⇄ beside a callout (D1 s23).

**Stat styling (numbers-first grammar):**
- Big numerals: Arial/Verdana bold, 44–60pt (sz 4400/6000 in XML), format "6+", "27%", "~23", "22+", "32+", "200+".
- D1: numeral in green on a light-green tint block (F2F8E5-family) or plain green; label 16pt Sakkal Majalla beside it.
- **D2 s27 deviation: stats in Deloitte ORANGE ED8B00 (×8 runs, sz 6000/4800)** — orange is used when the number is a *pain/scale* signal rather than an achievement. C00000 (red) appears twice for negative accents.
- Evidence variety per deck: D1 s21 = mega-project logo wall (Qiddiya, Expo 2030, King Salman Airport…) + population stats with green triangle marker; D1 s23 = real org-chart screenshot in a bordered frame + tinted takeaway box «تتطلب القرارات الاستراتيجية... تنسيقًا أفقيًا بين هذه الجهات، وليس معالجة قطاعية منفصلة» (light-green fill EFF3E8-family, dark green text); D1 s25 = rounded light-blue (E5F0F9) pill boxes «مركز الحكومة», «جهات قطاعية وهيئات»; D2 s27 = 3 giant orange stats (22+ إدارة, 32+ قسم, 3 مكاتب مدينتي); D2 s25 = 3 green/teal rounded-square rows (الأمانة/القطاعات/مراكز مدينتي — fills bright green 86BC25, teal 0D8390, teal) each with circled white icon and role sentence; D2 s29 = 4 diamond-chevron list of demand sources.
- Source line bottom-right of white half: «المصدر: أبحاث وتحليلات مونيتور ديلويت، أمانة منطقة الرياض» — Sakkal Majalla 8pt (sz 800) gray.
- «غير شامل» (non-exhaustive) white-outline pill appears when lists are illustrative (D2 s29; «توضيحي /غير شامل» D2 s27) — 10–11pt white text in thin white rounded-rect stroke.

**DECK deltas:** structure identical; only evidence objects & palette accent (green vs orange) change. The three-row implication panel with green-highlight phrases is constant.

## 4. ARCHETYPE D — PIVOT slide «ولتحقيق الممكنات، ينبغي أن تعمل ... على N مجالات رئيسية»
(D1 s26 N=4; D2 s30 N=3)

**Narrative role:** the hinge of the whole proposal. Converts diagnosed drivers into N numbered WORK AREAS which become (a) the tab-bar labels for every following methodology slide and (b) the approach tracks/مسارات later in the deck. N is recomputed per RFP (4 vs 3 observed).

**Layout anatomy:**
- Dark full-bleed background (open-book photo D1; dark silk D2).
- Top: 2 teal banner boxes (D1) or 3 (D2) — rounded rects, fill deep teal (004F59 seen in XML), white bold 12–13pt text, each with a small white icon; they carry a run-on "meta sentence" split across the banners, each fragment ending/starting with «...»: D1 right banner «يجب أن يتم توفير أساس تحليلي متكامل يدعم اتخاذ القرار، ويضمن وضوح الخيارات وقابليتها للتنفيذ ...» left banner «... وفي نفس الوقت يتم تعزيز القدرات المؤسسية وتمكين التطبيق المتسق للقرارات، مع ضمان مرونتها واستدامتها في ظل التحولات المستقبلية». D2 splits into 3 sequenced fragments: «أوّلًا، يجب وضع نموذح تشغيلي واضح للقطاع ...» / «... بعد ذلك، يجب تطوير آليات متابعة الأداء ..» / «...ويجب إدارة الأداء بشكل مستمر لضمان جودة المخرجات» — sequencing words أولاً/بعد ذلك/و mark logical order of the areas.
- Below: N columns RTL right→left = area 1→N. Each column: **huge ghost numeral (44pt, sz 4400, light gray D0D0CE-ish, regular weight)** → area name (Sakkal Majalla bold 18pt white, 2 lines) → **teal circle icon badge (0D8390 fill, white icon, broken white ring)** overlapping → **white card** (~3in wide) with 16pt dark-gray body text → **green 86BC25 underline strip at card bottom edge** (thin rect ~0.07in).
- Area names D1: 1 تمكين اتخاذ القرارات الاستراتيجية والاقتصادية / 2 رفع جاهزية القرارات وجودة المخرجات / 3 بناء التمكين المؤسسي وتعزيز القدرات / 4 الاستعداد للتحولات المستقبلية وتعزيز المرونة. D2: 1 صياغة النموذج التشغيلي ونموذج الحوكمة / 2 بناء آليات إدارة الأداء / 3 إدارة الأداء ودعم القرار.
- Card body = what Deloitte will DO in that area, future/gerund phrasing: «إعداد دراسات استراتيجية واقتصادية معمقة ووثائق سياسات متخصصة تسهم في توجيه القرارات...», «تحليل الاتجاهات بعيدة المدى، وبناء سيناريوهات استراتيجية، ودعم الإدارة العليا في اتخاذ قرارات مرنة وقابلة للتكيف مع المتغيرات».
- «غير شامل» pill top-left on D2 s30. Source line bottom-right. Footer standard.

**Mapping rule:** area k does NOT map 1:1 to driver k; instead areas 1..N-1 answer the drivers and the last area is always a forward-looking/institutional one (المرونة/التمكين المؤسسي D1; إدارة الأداء المستمرة D2). First area is always the "core analytical foundation" one.

## 5. NAVIGATION DEVICE — the area tab bar (runs s27–42)

From the slide AFTER the pivot until the proof section, every slide carries a top strip of N tabs = the N area names (plus, in D1 proof section s37–42, a SECOND tab set = the 4 differentiator names from s36 — the device is reused for the proof chapter).
- Implementation: rotated "Double Bracket" (bracketPair) shapes, one per area, text Sakkal Majalla ~11pt (sz 1100) white; INACTIVE tabs = dark charcoal fill, low-contrast text; **ACTIVE tab = teal fill (0D8390/004F59 family) with white/near-white bold text** (D2 renders show active tab clearly teal, e.g. s31–35 «صياغة النموذج التشغيلي ونموذج الحوكمة» active, then s35–42 «بناء آليات إدارة الأداء» / «إدارة الأداء» take over).
- Tabs are ordered RTL right→left in area order. The active tab moves strictly left as the deck advances — a progress bar made of words.
- D1 quirk: on s27–35 the same names appear as 4 floating labels at very top (rendered slightly clipped); on s37–42 the four differentiator labels («فهمنا لمتطلبات سمو الامين وطريقة إعداد العروض التنفيذية», «خبرة واسعة في التعامل ودعم طلبات المكاتب التنفيذية», «خبرة في مدينة الرياض والتعامل مع الجهات المحلية», «قدرات متعددة التخصصات مدعومة بشراكات عالمية») replace them with the same active-teal treatment.
- Reconstruction: place the tab group on each slide (not the layout — XML confirms tabs live in slide bodies), toggling active fill per chapter.

## 11. RECONSTRUCTION RECIPE — generalized PoV-argument formula for ANY new RFP

Step 1 — Derive N drivers (always 3): mine the RFP's scope + context sections for (a) strategic-alignment tension, (b) integration/coordination or standardization gap, (c) output-quality/velocity pressure. These three archetypal municipal-consulting drivers cover both observed decks; rename them in client vocabulary (4–7-word noun phrases). Pick valence: enablers (ممكنات) if the RFP is aspirational/strategic, challenges (تحديات) if remedial/operational.
Step 2 — Build Archetype A once (exact template geometry above); write 1 sentence per driver = current dynamic + «ما يفرض/يتطلب» need clause. Choose 3 thematic photos + 3 matching icons.
Step 3 — For each driver, clone→statement slide (ordinal title, dim others; write NOTHING new) then author the evidence slide: gather 2–4 verifiable facts (project counts, org units, population, request volumes) from client research; render as big-numeral stats/logo walls/org-chart screenshots on the white half; write 3–4 implication rows on the dark half, each with one green-highlighted capability phrase — those phrases must foreshadow your scope items. End the right title open («...») and start the left kicker with «... مما يتطلب».
Step 4 — Pivot slide: cluster the 9–12 green capability phrases from step 3 into N work areas (3–5; last = institutional/continuity). Order them as an execution sequence and encode it in the banner run-on sentence (أولاً... بعد ذلك... و...). These names are now IMMUTABLE: they become the tab bar and later the approach tracks/مسارات — verify the approach chapter uses identical strings.
Step 5 — Methodology previews: for each area select 1–4 house frameworks from the reference library (policy cycle, TOM house, EVM, BPMN, RASCI, risk 4-step, scenario planning, dashboard suite...). Redraw each as a native diagram (shapes, not screenshots), keep Latin names in parentheses, tag illustrative ones «للتوضيح», use (k/N) breadcrumb titles for example series, and set the tab bar active state. Sequence per framework family: what it is → what each step does → what questions it answers.
Step 6 — Proof ladder (most-specific → broadest): this-client artifacts (exec-brief gallery with real cover screenshots + «++ مستندات اخرى») → this-client project medallions with «قيد التنفيذ» pills → 15+ domain claim with ✓-badged logo wall + engagement one-liners → enumerated artifact flex if available (24-KPI-style) → alliance ring (if consortium) → 200+ expert wall. Reuse library slides verbatim where the client overlaps; recompute only claims that the new domain changes.
Step 7 — One-team close: bright daylight skyline, «وسنعمل كفريق واحد مع [client] لتحقيق الاهداف والأثر المرجو من المشروع», 4 ghost-numbered impacts = end-states of the N work areas (تحقيق/تمكين/بناء/الارتقاء + long-term horizon), green 046A38 emphasis.
Quality bar (what makes it read human-consultant-made): every title a complete argued sentence that chains to its neighbors with و/مما/بعد ذلك; one idea per slide but high evidence density on that idea; all numbers sourced («المصدر:...») and hedged («غير شامل») when illustrative; RTL numbering and strict grid reuse (identical EMU across sibling slides); real screenshots of real artifacts, never stock illustrations of "data"; the SAME words for work areas everywhere they recur; and a single light-switch moment (dark → daylight) at the close.
